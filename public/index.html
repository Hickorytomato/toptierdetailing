What I do

Everything happens at your place. You just need a spot where I can get all the way around the car.

Full detail

Hand wash and dry, wheels and tires, glass and trim. Inside, I vacuum everything, wipe down every surface, clean and condition the leather, and do the windows.

Paint correction

Machine polishing that takes out swirl marks, haze, and light scratches, so the paint looks deep and glossy again instead of dull.

Quoted after I see the paint

Ceramic coating

A hard, glossy layer that bonds to the paint. Water beads off, dirt doesn't stick, and it lasts far longer than wax. Best done right after correction.

From $499 with correction

Wheel polish

I take the brake dust and road film off the faces and barrels, then polish the wheels so the metal shines again.

Quoted by wheel

Good to know

Before you book

Do I need to be home?

Just for a few minutes at the start and end, so you can hand me the keys and do the walk-around. In between, you can go about your day.

What do I need to provide?

A spot where I can get all the way around the car. If I need water or an outlet, I'll ask when I confirm. Please take valuables out of the car beforehand.

What if it rains?

I'll text you and we'll pick another day. There's no charge to move it.

Is there a deposit?

Not for a full detail. Ceramic coating jobs take a deposit to hold the day, and I'll tell you the amount when I confirm.

How long does it take?

A full detail runs about 2–4 hours, depending on size and condition. Correction and ceramic take most of a day.

Ready when you are.

Pick a day, and I'll text you to confirm.
